NEUROBODYWORK

NeuroBodywork is 25+ years of advanced massage and manual therapy training combined with muscle testing to assist with neurological evaluation to reset dysfunctions with the central nervous system.

With many muscular skeletal injuries being ruled by the nervous system, it’s important to look at how the brain uses compensation pattern to correct these related dysfunctions rather than treating only the symptoms.

Often times the area of pain and the area of dysfunction are completely different.

MODALITIES

PDTR

Proprioceptive Deep Tendon Reflex Therapy (P-DTR) is a revolutionary neuromuscular treatment method that addresses a largely overlooked system of the body: the proprioceptive system. The proprioceptor system gives our brain information about what’s going on inside and outside of our body

Active Release Techniques®

Active Release Techniques® (ART®) is a hands-on touch therapy administered by trained professionals to assess and treat soft-tissue injuries. Soft tissue refers primarily to muscles, tendons, ligaments, fascia, and nerves.
